How PairWise works

1. Build a hierarchy: your goal, criteria, subcriteria, and alternatives.

2. Compare two items at a time. Criteria are compared by importance; alternatives are compared by preference or performance. One judgment fills two reciprocal matrix cells.

3. Understand local and global weights, the final ranking, consistency, contributions, and sensitivity.

A weight describes influence, not performance. The highest-ranked alternative is a recommendation produced by your judgments—not an objectively correct answer.
